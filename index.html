Tell Me More

The Challenge

Learning music doesn’t happen in a one-hour lesson each week. Young musicians spend most of their musical journey between lessons, practicing what they have learned. But practicing alone can sometimes feel repetitive, lonely, or difficult to stay motivated.

Vision

We provide a social and encouraging space where young people can come together and practice through playing. Those who take formal music lessons can use the club as a place to reinforce what they are learning, gain more playing experience, and enjoy music alongside other young musicians. For young people who are not yet taking music lessons, the club can also be a first spark by giving them a chance to try instruments, play with others, and experience the joy of making music, we hope to inspire a deeper interest that may lead them to pursue further music education.

Discover the Joy of Music

Learn to play the ukulele in a fun, welcoming space that sparks creativity and a lifelong love for music—no experience needed!.

Connect Through Community

Make lasting friendships and be part of a supportive community where youth grow together, inspire one another, and make a positive impact.

Grow as a Leader

Build confidence and develop leadership skills through group activities, performances, and collaborative projects designed to empower every young voice.

Participation in the Ukulele Club is completely free. Members are encouraged to bring their own ukuleles, but are also welcome to bring other instruments of their choice to contribute to a diverse and inclusive musical experience.

Every Beat has a Story.

  • June 2024

    Our Humble Beginnings

    Started With a Simple Idea. What if music practice didn’t always have to happen alone? In 2024, what began as a small group of three young people getting together to make music has grown into a community of young musicians. The idea was simple: practice becomes more enjoyable when you have people to practice with. Today, the club continues to grow as a place where young people can learn from one another, gain confidence through playing together, and discover that music can be both personal and social.
